Privacy.

Effective 2 September 2026 · Last updated 2 September 2026

Your audio leaves your device to be transcribed, and we do not keep it. Your transcript is cleaned up by a language model, and we do not keep that either unless you keep a history. We do not train anything on what you say, we do not sell anything to anyone, and privacy mode turns off every kind of saving. The rest of this page is the detail.

Status

Wisper Note has not published a public build yet, so there is nobody’s data to hold today. This policy is published now because it is the contract the product is being built to, and because an extension store requires a live policy before it will review anything. Where a section describes something that has not shipped, it says so in that section rather than in a footnote.

1.What this covers

This policy covers:

  • The Wisper Note apps for macOS, Windows, iPhone and Android
  • The Wisper Note Chrome extension
  • wispernote.com

It is written by the people who build Wisper Note. Where it says “we”, it means them and nobody else.

2.What leaves your device, and when

Dictation is not local. When you hold the dictation key, this is what goes to a server, and it is the whole list:

  • Your audio, only while the dictation key is held
  • The resulting transcript
  • The identifier of the app you are typing into — its bundle id or process name, not its contents
  • Your dictionary terms, sent as transcription hints

And this is what does not:

  • Anything you did not dictate
  • The contents of the app you are typing into
  • Your keystrokes
  • Your screen
  • Audio written to disk — it is held in memory and zeroed on stop, in every mode

The audio stream opens when you press the key and closes when you let go. Nothing is captured between one dictation and the next.

3.Who processes it

The disclosure, in full

When you dictate, Wisper Note streams your audio to Deepgram, Inc. to be transcribed, and sends the resulting text to Anthropic PBC to be cleaned up. Your account, dictionary, snippets and any history you keep are stored with Supabase, Inc. Audio is never written to disk and we do not keep it.

That paragraph is the same string Wisper Note shows in the desktop app’s Privacy pane and on the Chrome extension’s first-run screen. It is one string in one file, so the three cannot drift apart.

4.What we store, and for how long

Audio

Audio is never written to disk, on any platform, in any mode. It is held in memory while you are holding the key and zeroed when you let go.

Everything else is a row in a database, and there are nine tables. This is all of them.

5.Privacy mode

Privacy mode is a single switch. While it is on:

  • Nothing written to history
  • No audio kept, ever
  • No learned terms
  • No usage events

And here is what it does not do:

Privacy mode stops us saving anything. It does not stop your audio leaving your device — transcription still happens on Deepgram's servers. There is no offline mode in this version.

6.Training

We do not train models on your audio, your transcripts, your dictionary or your notes, and we do not let anyone else. No person reads your transcripts.

That is a statement about us. The companies in section 3 publish their own terms, and this page does not restate them — see the note under that table.

7.Analytics and crash reporting

There is no third-party analytics SDK in the desktop app, the mobile apps or the extension, and none on this website. No product analytics service, no session recorder, no advertising pixel.

What the product counts is in the usage_counters row of the table above: how many words and how many dictations, per period. Counts, not content. Your transcripts are never part of an analytics event.

Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on, and the switch is in the app’s Privacy settings beside privacy mode. Neither the crash reporter nor its switch has shipped yet; when it does, this section names the exact toggle and exactly what a crash report contains.

8.Cookies

wispernote.com sets no cookies. There is no advertising cookie, no analytics cookie and no session cookie on the marketing site.

One thing is stored in your browser: a key called wn-theme in localStorage, holding light or dark when you pick a theme in the footer. It never leaves your browser and it is removed when you set the theme back to System. The site loads no third-party script, no third-party stylesheet and no web font.

Open your browser’s network panel on this page and check. This section is meant to be checkable, and it is written from the built output rather than from intent.

9.Your rights and how to use them

You can get a copy of everything we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and everything attached to it. Deleting the account removes every row in the table above and every stored object that belongs to you.

Those are buttons, not requests: Export my data and Delete account live in the account dashboard. Neither the dashboard nor the deletion test that proves it works has shipped, so this section does not yet tell you where to click. It will say the exact location the day both ship, and not before — a rights section that names a button which does not exist is worse than one that admits the gap.

Until then, the way to reach us is section 13, and the honest state of that section is written there.

10.Children

Wisper Note is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11.International transfers

Your data is processed by the companies in section 3, and they operate in more than one country.

We have not yet published the list of regions your audio and your text are processed in, and we are not going to guess at it on a privacy policy. It is the last thing outstanding on this page, it is tracked as owner item DP-4, and it is resolved by reading each processor’s current terms and recording the answer with a date before this policy goes to an extension store.

12.Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the change is listed on the changelog like any other release note. A change that widens what we collect or who receives it is announced in the app and by email at least 30 days before it takes effect, and you can close your account before it does.

13.Contact
