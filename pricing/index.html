Why the free plan is not crippled.

The free plan gets the same cleanup, the same tone and the same dictionary. A crippled free plan is a bad demonstration of the product, and the free plan is the demonstration. We cap the volume and the surface — words, devices, history retention — because those scale with real use instead of stopping you mid-sentence.

The cap is 2,000 words a week — about 20 short emails. That referent is our own arithmetic at about 100 words per short email, and if you ask us we will say so. It is not a measurement of anyone’s usage, and we are not going to present it as one.

Questions.

How do I cancel?

Account → Billing → Cancel. Two clicks, in the Stripe portal. It takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for.

What happens to my stuff if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. Wisper Note goes back to the free plan and keeps working; you get the weekly cap back, and sync stops.

Do you offer refunds?

We have not published a refund policy yet, so this page does not state one. It will say 14 days when the terms say 14 days and the process exists, and not a day before. Nothing on this page can charge you today.

Does the free plan expire?

No. There is no trial and no countdown. The cap resets every Monday.

What counts as a word?

The words in the text we insert, counted after cleanup. Words you delete afterwards still count; we have no way to know you deleted them.

What does “unlimited” mean?

No weekly cap. There is a per-session length limit of 15 minutes and an anti-abuse ceiling described in the terms, and neither will be reached by a person dictating.

Where does my audio go?

To Deepgram to be transcribed, and the text to Anthropic to be cleaned up. Nothing is kept. The full answer.

Do I need an account?

Wisper Note needs a free account to transcribe. You'll make one inside the app — the download isn't gated.
