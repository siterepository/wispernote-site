# Wisper Note — pre-launch crawl policy.
#
# The marketing pages stay out of the index until there is a product to
# download. Ranking a page that says "there is nothing to download yet" spends
# the first crawl on the wrong impression, and the flip costs one line here.
#
# The two legal documents are the exception, and deliberately so. Both the
# Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons require a publicly reachable privacy policy
# URL naming our processors before they will accept a submission, and a blanket
# Disallow makes that URL uncrawlable no matter what the page's own meta robots
# tag says — the crawler never gets far enough to read it. /privacy and /terms
# carry `index: true` in their own metadata; this file has to agree.
#
# At launch: delete the Disallow line, keep the Sitemap line.

User-agent: *
Allow: /privacy/
Allow: /terms/
Disallow: /

Sitemap: https://wispernote.com/sitemap.xml
