Including the rows where we lose, and the rows where we could not check.

How to read this table.

Yes
the vendor's own site says so. We read it on the date shown.
Partial
the vendor's own site says so with a limit or a qualification.
Unverified
we could not find it on the pages we read. That is not a “no”. It means we did not check, or could not.

No cell says “no” unless we can quote the vendor denying it. Every cell links to the page we read and the date we read it. If we have got something wrong we will fix it and say we did — there is no monitored address to report it to yet, and it goes here the day there is.

Reasons to buy someone else’s.

A comparison page that cannot name a reason to buy a competitor is not a comparison page.

What Wispr Flow has that we do not.

A meeting notetaker, a team plan with a shared dictionary, and SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance work. If you need any of those, buy theirs.

What Superwhisper has that we do not.

Transcription that runs entirely on your Mac with no connection at all, and a one-time licence. If you will not send audio anywhere, buy theirs.

What the free dictation on your machine has that we do not.

It is free, it is already installed, and on a Mac it runs on device. If it is enough for you, it is enough.
